Science in plain language

Energy is the ability to cause change. Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space. The scientific method: question, hypothesis, test, data, revise.

  • Observe carefully and write what you see.
  • Separate opinion from measurement.
  • Cite sources when you claim a fact.

History & civic literacy
